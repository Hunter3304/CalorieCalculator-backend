# Body Circumference Tracking and Production Release

Date: 2026-07-27
Documentation finalized: 2026-08-04
Status: Completed

## Requested outcome

Add body-circumference records alongside food and body-weight records. The homepage needed a matching green action, six centimeter inputs, independent carry-forward, and a trend widget whose dropdown selects the displayed measurement.

The final six measurements are chest, waist, hip, arm, thigh, and calf. Fields may be partially filled or all blank. A never-recorded value displays `--`.

## Product behavior

- Circumference belongs to the selected non-future date.
- Every field is optional, positive, and limited to one decimal place.
- Sparse values carry forward independently from their latest earlier source.
- An all-blank new save is a successful no-op and creates no empty row.
- Clearing an explicit selected-date field restores inheritance from an earlier value.
- Deleting removes the complete selected-date sparse record.
- The homepage displays all six effective values.
- The trend page defaults to waist, offers all six measurements, and supports rolling 7-, 30-, and 365-day ranges.
- Trend points before a measurement's first record remain blank; later missing dates carry forward.
